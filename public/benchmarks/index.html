500 / 2,000-switch stress comparison

Five technique scenes, including a native unanimated reference. All controls change state together, twice per trial. Same 1920×1440 viewport and 200ms motion; five trials per case.

These graphs do not rank whole libraries. The edge, transform, and grid scenes are normalized illustrations. Transform/grid do not reproduce Slidebox's deformation. FPS means rAF callback cadence, not actual GPU-presented frames. This is a local headed Chromium pilot, not a device-wide or cross-browser performance claim.
