500 / 2,000-switch stress comparison

Five technique scenes, including a native unanimated reference. All controls change state together, twice per trial. Same 1920×1440 viewport and 200ms motion; five trials per case.

These graphs do not rank whole libraries. The edge, transform, and grid scenes are normalized illustrations. Transform/grid do not reproduce Slidebox's deformation. FPS means rAF callback cadence, not actual GPU-presented frames. This is a local headless Chromium pilot, not a device-wide or cross-browser performance claim.

Read timing and work together: less layout time in a low-FPS case can mean fewer animation frames ran, not that the implementation became more efficient. Zero layout counters also do not guarantee smooth compositor or GPU output. The browser reported: ANGLE (Google, Vulkan 1.3.0 (SwiftShader Device (Subzero) (0x0000C0DE)), SwiftShader driver).
